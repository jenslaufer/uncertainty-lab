# Against the Gods

Peter Bernstein — 1996

## Three key theories

1. **Mastery of risk defines modernity** — Bernstein opens with the claim that the mastery of risk marks the boundary between modern times and the past: the future is more than a whim of the gods. Measuring risk let people take it deliberately. Insurance, investment and enterprise followed.
2. **From counting chances to regression to the mean** — The book traces how Pascal, Fermat, Bernoulli, Bayes, Gauss and Galton turned chance into measurement. Sampling, the bell curve and regression to the mean gave decisions a numerical basis. Galton's regression to the mean gets special weight for investors.
3. **Utility and its limits** — Daniel Bernoulli showed that the value of a gain depends on the person: utility diminishes with wealth. The last part of the book asks whether people choose rationally at all. Kahneman and Tversky's prospect theory shows how real choices depart from rational models.
