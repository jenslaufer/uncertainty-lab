# Thinking, Fast and Slow

Daniel Kahneman — 2011

## Three key theories

1. **System 1 and System 2** — Two modes of thought: System 1 runs fast, automatic, and intuitive; System 2 runs slow, effortful, and deliberate. System 2 is lazy and often endorses System 1's answers. Many errors arise when System 1 answers and no one checks.
2. **Heuristics and biases (WYSIATI)** — Intuition substitutes easy questions for hard ones and builds stories from whatever information is at hand: "what you see is all there is". Anchoring, availability, base-rate neglect, and overconfidence follow. Judgment is systematically, not randomly, wrong.
3. **Prospect theory** — People value gains and losses relative to a reference point, not final wealth. Losses loom larger than gains, and sensitivity diminishes with size. Combined with the overweighting of small probabilities, this explains risk-seeking in losses and risk-aversion in gains.
