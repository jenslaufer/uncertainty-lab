# Skin in the Game

Nassim Taleb

## Three key theories

1. **Symmetry of risk** — Those who make decisions must bear their downside. Hammurabi's code and the Silver Rule enforce this symmetry. Without it, risk shifts to others and systems fail to learn.
2. **The minority rule** — An intransigent minority can impose its preferences on a flexible majority. Kosher and peanut-free foods spread this way. Aggregate behavior cannot be read from the average member.
3. **Ergodicity and ruin** — Ensemble probability, across many people, differs from time probability, along one life. Any exposure to ruin makes averages meaningless for the individual. Rationality means survival: avoid ruin first.
