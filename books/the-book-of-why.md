# The Book of Why

Judea Pearl & Dana Mackenzie — 2018

## Three key theories

1. **Ladder of causation** — Three rungs: association (seeing), intervention (doing), counterfactuals (imagining). Data alone reach only the first rung. Machines and statistics that stop there cannot answer causal questions.
2. **Causal diagrams and the do-calculus** — Arrows encode assumptions about what causes what. The do-operator separates intervention from observation; the back-door and front-door criteria say when confounding can be removed and an effect computed from observational data.
3. **Counterfactuals and mediation** — Structural models let you ask what would have happened otherwise. This grounds attribution, responsibility and the split of an effect into direct and indirect paths. Pearl calls this shift the Causal Revolution.
