# Hidden Order

John Holland — 1995

## Three key theories

1. **Complex adaptive systems** — Systems such as cities, immune systems and economies consist of many agents that adapt to each other. Their coherence persists without central control. Holland sets out to find general principles common to all of them.
2. **Seven basics: four properties and three mechanisms** — Every complex adaptive system shows aggregation, nonlinearity, flows and diversity, and uses tagging, internal models and building blocks. Tags let agents select partners and form aggregates. Building blocks let agents model a novel world by recombining known parts.
3. **Adaptive agents as rule systems** — Holland models agents as sets of if–then rules (classifier systems). Credit assignment (the bucket brigade) rewards rules that lead to success; a genetic algorithm creates new rules by recombination. The Echo model puts such agents into a simulated world to study how ecosystems and economies emerge.
