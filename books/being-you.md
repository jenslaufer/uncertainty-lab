# Being You

Anil Seth — 2021

## Three key theories

1. **The real problem of consciousness** — Instead of the hard problem, Seth asks how to explain, predict, and control the properties of conscious experience in terms of brain mechanisms. Progress on the real problem may dissolve the hard one, as vitalism dissolved in biology. Measures of conscious level, such as perturbational complexity, illustrate the approach.
2. **Perception as controlled hallucination** — The brain generates predictions about the causes of sensory signals; experience is those predictions, reined in by prediction error. We do not see the world as it is but as it is useful to us. Hallucination is uncontrolled perception.
3. **The beast machine** — The deepest layer of selfhood rests on predictions of the body's interior (interoception). These predictions serve control: keeping the organism alive within physiological bounds. Emotions and the sense of being a self flow from this regulation, which ties consciousness to life rather than to intelligence.
