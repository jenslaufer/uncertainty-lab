# A Brief History of Intelligence

Max Bennett — 2023

## Three key theories

1. **Five breakthroughs** — Brain evolution proceeded through five breakthroughs: steering, reinforcing, simulating, mentalizing, and speaking. Each built on the one before and solved a new problem. Understanding the sequence explains the architecture of the human brain and what AI still lacks.
2. **Reinforcing via temporal difference learning** — Early vertebrates learned by trial and error. Dopamine signals a reward prediction error, matching the temporal difference algorithm from AI. This let animals learn from the expected value of future outcomes, not just immediate rewards.
3. **Simulating with a generative model** — The mammalian neocortex learned a model of the world and could imagine outcomes before acting. Vicarious trial and error, counterfactual reasoning, and episodic memory follow from simulation. Primates extended it to model other minds (mentalizing).
