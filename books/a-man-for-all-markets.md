# A Man for All Markets

Edward Thorp — 2017

## Three key theories

1. **Beat the dealer with card counting** — Blackjack is not independent trials: removed cards shift the odds. By tracking the remaining deck, a player knows when the edge turns positive. Thorp proved this by computer analysis and then in Nevada casinos.
2. **Size bets with Kelly** — An edge is worthless if bet sizes ruin you. Thorp applied the Kelly criterion to blackjack and later to his funds: bet a fraction of capital proportional to the edge. This maximizes long-run growth while avoiding ruin.
3. **Hedge away risk, keep the edge** — Thorp priced warrants and options before Black–Scholes and hedged mispriced ones against the stock (delta hedging). Princeton Newport Partners ran these market-neutral arbitrages for years. He also argues that most investors should hold low-cost index funds, since few have a real edge.
