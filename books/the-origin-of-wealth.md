# The Origin of Wealth

Eric Beinhocker — 2006

## Three key theories

1. **Complexity Economics vs. Traditional Economics** — Neoclassical economics models the economy as a closed equilibrium system. Beinhocker argues it is an open, dynamic, non-equilibrium complex adaptive system with heterogeneous, boundedly rational agents and networks of interaction.
2. **Wealth as fit order** — Wealth is fit order: low-entropy arrangements of matter, energy and information that people value. Creating it consumes energy, consistent with thermodynamics.
3. **Evolution as the engine of growth** — Wealth emerges from an evolutionary algorithm of differentiate, select, amplify. It runs on three co-evolving design spaces: Physical Technologies, Social Technologies and Business Plans. Markets select plans; firms and strategies must adapt continuously.
