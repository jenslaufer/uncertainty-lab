# Risk, Uncertainty and Profit

Frank Knight — 1921

## Three key theories

1. **Risk vs. uncertainty** — Risk is measurable: outcomes follow known or estimable probabilities and can be insured. Uncertainty is unmeasurable: situations are too unique to group into classes. This distinction became known as Knightian uncertainty.
2. **Profit as reward for bearing uncertainty** — Under perfect competition with only measurable risk, profit vanishes; insurance and pooling absorb risk. True profit exists only because of uncertainty. It rewards judgment in situations no calculation can price.
3. **Uncertainty creates the enterprise** — Confident, venturesome people take responsibility for uncertain outcomes. They guarantee wages to others and keep the residual. The firm and the entrepreneur arise as a response to uncertainty.
