# Probability Theory: The Logic of Science

E. T. Jaynes — 2003

## Three key theories

1. **Probability as extended logic** — Probability extends deductive logic to reasoning under incomplete information. From a few desiderata of consistent plausible reasoning, Cox's theorems derive the product and sum rules. Bayesian inference follows as the only consistent method.
2. **Maximum entropy** — Assign the distribution that maximizes entropy subject to what you know. It encodes your information and nothing more. Maxent gives a principled way to build priors and links inference to statistical mechanics.
3. **Mind projection fallacy** — Probabilities describe a state of knowledge, not a property of the world. Treating our uncertainty as physical randomness confuses epistemology with ontology. Jaynes uses this to attack frequentist dogma and paradoxes.
