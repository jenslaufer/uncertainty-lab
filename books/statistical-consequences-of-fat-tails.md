# Statistical Consequences of Fat Tails

Nassim Taleb — 2020

## Three key theories

1. **Preasymptotics and the slow law of large numbers** — Under fat tails the law of large numbers works too slowly to help. Taleb's kappa metric measures how many more observations a fat-tailed variable needs than a Gaussian one. Real data live in the preasymptotic zone.
2. **Conventional statistics break** — Sample means, variance, correlation, regression and R² become unreliable or meaningless under fat tails. A few observations dominate the moments. Methods built for thin tails give false confidence.
3. **Exposure over forecasting** — Distinguish the variable x from your payoff f(x). You cannot predict x in Extremistan, but you can shape f(x). Work on exposure, not on estimating probabilities.
