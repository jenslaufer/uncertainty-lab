# The Success Equation

Michael Mauboussin — 2012

## Three key theories

1. **The skill–luck continuum** — Every activity sits somewhere between pure luck (roulette) and pure skill (chess). Outcomes combine both. Mauboussin estimates the position from the variance of observed results minus the variance luck alone would produce.
2. **The paradox of skill** — As competitors grow more skilled and more uniform, the spread of skill narrows. Luck then decides more outcomes, even though absolute skill rises. This explains why top performance in investing or baseball hitting has become harder to sustain.
3. **Reversion to the mean** — The more luck shapes an outcome, the stronger the pull back to the average. Extreme results in luck-heavy fields predict little about the next round. Judge decisions by process, not by single outcomes.
