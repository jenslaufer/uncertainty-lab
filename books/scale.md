# Scale

Geoffrey West — 2017

## Three key theories

1. **Quarter-power scaling from networks** — Metabolic rate scales with body mass to the 3/4 power (Kleiber's law). West traces this to space-filling, hierarchical networks that supply every cell. Network geometry, not biological detail, sets the rules of life span, pace and size.
2. **Superlinear scaling of cities** — Double a city's population and socioeconomic outputs (wages, patents, crime) rise about 15% more than double. Infrastructure scales sublinearly and saves about 15%. Social networks drive increasing returns.
3. **Finite-time singularity and the innovation treadmill** — Superlinear growth heads toward a singularity in finite time. Only a major innovation resets the clock, and each reset must come faster. Companies, by contrast, scale like organisms: sublinear, then they die.
