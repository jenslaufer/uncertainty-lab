# Antifragile

Nassim Taleb

## Three key theories

1. **The triad: fragile, robust, antifragile** — Fragile things break under volatility, robust things resist it, antifragile things gain from it. Bones, evolution and some economies improve under stress. Antifragility is the missing opposite of fragility.
2. **Fragility as concavity** — Fragility is a concave response to stressors: harm accelerates as shocks grow. Antifragility is convexity: more upside than downside from variation. Measure second-order effects instead of forecasting events.
3. **Optionality and the barbell** — Options let you keep upside and cap downside, so you need not predict. The barbell combines extreme safety with small, aggressive bets and avoids the fragile middle. Tinkering with options beats top-down planning.
