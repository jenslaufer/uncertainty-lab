# The (Mis)behavior of Markets

Benoît Mandelbrot & Richard Hudson — 2004

## Three key theories

1. **Wild randomness, not mild** — Price changes follow fat-tailed, power-law distributions, not the bell curve. Mandelbrot found this in cotton prices in the 1960s. Standard finance underestimates crashes by orders of magnitude.
2. **The Noah and Joseph effects** — The Noah effect names sudden discontinuous jumps. The Joseph effect names long-term dependence: trends and clusters persist, measured by the Hurst exponent. Markets have memory and jump; the random walk assumes neither.
3. **Multifractal markets** — Price charts look alike at every time scale. Mandelbrot models this with multifractals and trading time, which stretches and compresses clock time with volatility. Scaling, not equilibrium, describes how markets move.
