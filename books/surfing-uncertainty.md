# Surfing Uncertainty

Andy Clark — 2016

## Three key theories

1. **Predictive processing** — The brain is a hierarchical prediction machine. Higher levels predict activity at lower levels; only the mismatch, the prediction error, flows upward. Perception is the brain's best guess, continuously corrected by the senses.
2. **Precision weighting** — The brain estimates how reliable each prediction error is and weights it accordingly. Attention is the optimization of this precision. Errors in precision estimation may underlie hallucinations, delusions, and autism-related differences.
3. **Action-oriented predictive processing** — Action is another way to reduce prediction error: the body moves to make the predicted sensations come true (active inference). Perception and action share one process. Cognition is embodied and exploits the body and world to keep predictions cheap.
