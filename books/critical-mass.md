# Critical Mass

Philip Ball — 2004

## Three key theories

1. **Physics of society** — Statistical physics explains collective human behavior. Individual choices vary, but aggregates follow law-like regularities, as molecules do in a gas. Ball traces this "physics of society" from Hobbes and the early social statisticians to modern modeling, and shows that social statistics inspired statistical physics, not the reverse.
2. **Phase transitions and critical points** — Traffic, crowds, markets and opinion shift abruptly between states when a parameter crosses a threshold. Small nudges near a critical point trigger large collective change. Order arises without central control.
3. **Power laws and networks** — Near criticality and in growing networks, event sizes follow power laws: firm sizes, market crashes, city sizes, wars. Small-world and scale-free networks shape how influence and failure spread.
