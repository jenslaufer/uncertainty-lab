# Risk Savvy

Gerd Gigerenzer — 2014

## Three key theories

1. **Risk vs. uncertainty: less is more** — In a world of known risks, calculation works. Under uncertainty, simple heuristics often beat complex models because they do not overfit. The 1/N rule and the gaze heuristic show how.
2. **Risk literacy through transparent numbers** — Natural frequencies and absolute risks make statistics understandable; conditional probabilities and relative risks mislead. Doctors and patients misread test results without them. Gigerenzer argues anyone can learn to read risk.
3. **Defensive decision-making** — Professionals often choose the option that protects them from blame, not the best one. Defensive medicine orders needless tests and treatments. A culture of fear of error makes decisions worse.
