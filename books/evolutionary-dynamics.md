# Evolutionary Dynamics

Martin Nowak — 2006

## Three key theories

1. **Quasispecies and the error threshold** — Mutation and selection on sequence space form a cloud of variants, not a single best type. Above a critical mutation rate, the error threshold, selection cannot hold the population on a fitness peak and information is lost.
2. **Evolutionary game dynamics** — Fitness depends on what others do. Replicator dynamics, the Prisoner's Dilemma and spatial games show how strategies and cooperation rise and fall; spatial structure lets cooperators survive in clusters.
3. **Stochastic dynamics in finite populations** — The Moran process gives fixation probabilities of mutants under selection and drift. Evolutionary graph theory shows that population structure can amplify or suppress selection.
