# The Bed of Procrustes

Nassim Taleb

## Three key theories

1. **The Procrustean bed** — Procrustes cut or stretched guests to fit his bed. Taleb argues we do the same: we squeeze the world into reductive categories and reshape people to fit technology. He calls every aphorism in the book a Procrustean bed of sorts.
2. **Subtractive knowledge** — Knowing what is wrong is more robust than knowing what is right. Removing errors, harms and bad ideas beats adding theories. The book gives this via negativa its own section, "Epistemology and Subtractive Knowledge".
3. **Sucker problems and domain dependence** — Two sections, "Charming and Less Charming Sucker Problems" and "The Ludic Fallacy and Domain Dependence", mock those who trust narratives and forecasts. Knowledge that works in the classroom or in games fails in real life. The theme links to the ludic fallacy of *The Black Swan*.
