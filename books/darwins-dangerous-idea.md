# Darwin's Dangerous Idea

Daniel Dennett — 1995

## Three key theories

1. **Evolution as algorithm** — Natural selection is a substrate-neutral, mindless algorithm: variation, selection, heredity. Mechanical steps with no foresight yield designed-looking results. Anything that meets the conditions evolves.
2. **Universal acid** — Darwin's idea eats through every traditional concept it touches: life, mind, meaning, culture, ethics. Design no longer needs a designer. Dennett defends this reach against critics who would contain it.
3. **Cranes, not skyhooks, in Design Space** — Skyhooks are miraculous lifts from above; cranes are mechanisms built from below, such as sex, culture and minds. Evolution explores a vast Design Space (the "Library of Mendel") by cranes alone.
