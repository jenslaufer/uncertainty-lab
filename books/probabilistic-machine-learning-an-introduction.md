# Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction

Kevin Murphy — 2022

## Three key theories

1. **The probabilistic perspective** — Murphy treats every model as a probability distribution over unknowns, from linear regression to deep networks. Uncertainty about parameters and predictions is modeled explicitly. Maximum likelihood, MAP, and Bayesian inference become points on one spectrum.
2. **Bayesian decision theory** — Predictions are actions chosen to minimize expected loss under the posterior. The loss function, not just the model, determines the best decision. This links classification, regression, and model selection to one principle.
3. **Information theory as the language of learning** — Entropy, cross-entropy, KL divergence, and mutual information define what models optimize and how distributions differ. Minimizing cross-entropy is maximum likelihood. These quantities connect compression, inference, and learning.
