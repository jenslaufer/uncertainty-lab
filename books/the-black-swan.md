# The Black Swan

Nassim Taleb

## Three key theories

1. **Mediocristan vs. Extremistan** — In Mediocristan no single observation moves the total; height and weight live there. In Extremistan one observation can dominate; wealth, book sales and wars live there. Tools built for the first fail in the second.
2. **Black Swans and the narrative fallacy** — A Black Swan is rare, has extreme impact, and looks predictable only in hindsight. We build stories that make the past seem explicable and ignore silent evidence. This hides how much the world runs on the unforeseen.
3. **Ludic fallacy and the problem of induction** — Casino-style probability does not describe real life. The turkey, fed for a thousand days, grows most confident just before the slaughter. Past data cannot rule out the event that matters.
