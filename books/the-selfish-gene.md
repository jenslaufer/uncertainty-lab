# The Selfish Gene

Richard Dawkins — 1976

## Three key theories

1. **Gene's-eye view of evolution** — The gene, not the organism or the group, is the unit of selection. Genes are replicators; bodies are "survival machines" (vehicles) built to copy them. Selection favors genes that act in their own replication interest.
2. **Evolutionarily stable strategies** — Following Maynard Smith, behavior is a strategy in a game against other strategies. An ESS cannot be invaded by a rare mutant strategy. Aggression, mating and parental investment settle into such stable mixes.
3. **Altruism from selfish genes, and memes** — Kin selection and reciprocal altruism explain cooperation as gene self-interest. Dawkins extends replicator logic to culture: memes are units of cultural transmission that spread by copying, independent of their benefit to hosts.
