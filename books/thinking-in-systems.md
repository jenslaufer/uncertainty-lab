# Thinking in Systems

Donella Meadows — 2008

## Three key theories

1. **Stocks and flows** — A system is elements, interconnections and a purpose. Stocks accumulate; flows change them. Stocks buffer change and create delays, so systems respond slowly and overshoot.
2. **Feedback loops** — Balancing loops push a stock toward a goal; reinforcing loops amplify change in either direction. Shifting dominance between loops, plus delays, generates oscillation, growth and collapse. Behavior comes from structure, not from events.
3. **System traps and leverage points** — Recurring structures produce recurring failures: policy resistance, tragedy of the commons, drift to low performance, escalation, success to the successful. Meadows ranks places to intervene, from parameters (weak) to goals, paradigms and the power to transcend paradigms (strong).
