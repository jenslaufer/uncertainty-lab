# Algorithms to Live By

Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths — 2016

## Three key theories

1. **Optimal stopping** — When options arrive in sequence and you cannot go back, look first, then leap. The 37% rule: explore the first 37% of candidates, then take the first one better than all before. It turns "when to stop searching" into a solvable problem.
2. **Explore/exploit** — Every choice trades gathering information against using what you know. Multi-armed bandits, the Gittins index and upper confidence bounds formalize the tradeoff. Your time horizon decides the balance: explore when young, exploit near the end.
3. **Bayes's rule and priors** — Good predictions combine small data with the right prior. The shape of the prior sets the rule: multiplicative for power laws, average for normal, additive for Erlang. Knowing which distribution you face matters more than the data point.
