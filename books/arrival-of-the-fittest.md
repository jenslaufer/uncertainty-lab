# Arrival of the Fittest

Andreas Wagner — 2014

## Three key theories

1. **The arrival problem** — Natural selection explains the survival of the fittest, not their arrival. Selection preserves innovations; it does not create them. A theory of evolution needs a theory of how new phenotypes appear.
2. **Genotype networks in hyperastronomical libraries** — Metabolisms, regulatory circuits and proteins live in vast "libraries" of possible genotypes. Genotypes with the same phenotype form connected networks spanning the library. Populations drift along them without losing function.
3. **Robustness enables innovation** — Because many genotypes share a phenotype, mutations are often neutral. Neutral drift lets populations explore widely, and the neighborhoods of different network points hold many different new phenotypes. Robustness, not fragility, makes innovation possible.
