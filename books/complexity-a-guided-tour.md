# Complexity: A Guided Tour

Melanie Mitchell — 2009

## Three key theories

1. **Emergence from simple rules** — Large numbers of simple components, with no central control, produce complex collective behavior. Ant colonies, the immune system and cellular automata show it. Complexity lives in the interactions, not in the parts.
2. **Information processing in living systems** — Mitchell treats computation as a lens on nature. Ant colonies, cells and immune systems gather information, process it, and act on it. Information, entropy and computation give complex systems a common vocabulary.
3. **Network thinking and scaling** — Small-world and scale-free networks recur across biology, technology and society. Metabolic scaling laws suggest network structure shapes what systems can do. Shared structure lets insights transfer between fields.
