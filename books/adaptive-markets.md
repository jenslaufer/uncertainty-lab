# Adaptive Markets

Andrew Lo — 2017

## Three key theories

1. **The Adaptive Markets Hypothesis** — The Efficient Markets Hypothesis is not wrong but incomplete: it holds only in stable environments. Markets are an ecology of competing species — investors, funds, strategies. Efficiency varies with the number of competitors, the opportunities and how well participants have adapted.
2. **Behavior as evolved heuristics** — Biases are not irrational; they are adaptations that fit past environments. Lo draws on neuroscience to show how fear and greed drive financial decisions. When the environment shifts, once-useful heuristics misfire.
3. **Financial evolution at the speed of thought** — Natural selection acts on strategies and beliefs, not only genes. Strategies boom while they fit and fade when conditions change, so risk premia and returns are not stable. Lo derives behaviors such as probability matching and risk aversion from a simple evolutionary model.
