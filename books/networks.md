# Networks

Mark Newman — 2nd ed., 2018

## Three key theories

1. **Network measures** — Adjacency matrices and graph theory turn systems into measurable structure. Centrality (degree, eigenvector, betweenness), clustering, assortativity and community structure quantify who matters and how the network is organized.
2. **Network models and generating functions** — Random graphs, the configuration model and growth models with preferential attachment give analytic predictions. Generating functions compute component sizes, path lengths and the onset of a giant component.
3. **Processes on networks** — Percolation, epidemics and dynamical systems run on top of network structure. Percolation theory gives robustness under node removal; the degree distribution sets epidemic thresholds and the stability of dynamics.
