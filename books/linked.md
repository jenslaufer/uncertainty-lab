# Linked

Albert-László Barabási — 2002

## Three key theories

1. **Scale-free networks** — The Web, cells, citations and social ties do not follow random-graph statistics. Their degree distributions follow power laws: a few hubs hold most links. No typical node exists.
2. **Growth and preferential attachment** — Networks grow, and new nodes link preferentially to well-connected ones: the rich get richer. This mechanism generates hubs and power laws. A fitness term lets latecomers with high fitness overtake incumbents.
3. **Robust yet fragile** — Scale-free networks survive random failures because most nodes are minor. Targeted attacks on hubs shatter them: their Achilles' heel. The same hubs speed the spread of viruses and fads.
