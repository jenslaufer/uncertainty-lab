## Core concepts

Cross-cutting ideas that link the fields above. Suggested learning order; each comes with a starter experiment.

| # | Concept | Links | Experiment |
|---|---------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Bayesian updating | statistics, AI, neuroscience, investment | Update a coin's bias from flips; compare priors. |
| 2 | Fat tails & power laws | economy, networks, scaling, evolution | Sample Gaussian vs. Pareto; watch the sample mean converge — or not. |
| 3 | Convexity & nonlinearity (Jensen) | antifragility, investment | Apply convex and concave payoffs to the same noise; compare outcomes. |
| 4 | Ergodicity | economy, investment | Multiplicative coin game: positive expected value, yet most players go broke. Add Kelly sizing. |
| 5 | Explore vs. exploit | AI, evolution, neuroscience, investment | Multi-armed bandit: epsilon-greedy vs. UCB vs. Thompson sampling. |
| 6 | Information & entropy | computer science, AI, neuroscience | Compress text; relate compression ratio to entropy and predictability. |
| 7 | Feedback & dynamical systems | complexity, economy | Logistic map: from stable point to chaos. |
| 8 | Emergence | complexity, neuroscience, AI | Game of Life or boids: global patterns from local rules. |
| 9 | Networks & scaling | networks, scaling, economy | Grow a preferential-attachment network; fit its degree distribution. Simulate a cascade. |
| 10 | Optimization & selection | evolution, AI, economy | Genetic algorithm vs. gradient descent on the same landscape. |
| 11 | Causality | statistics, AI, economy | Simulate a confounder; show correlation without causation; fix it with adjustment. |
| 12 | Game theory & agents | evolution, economy, AI | Iterated prisoner's dilemma tournament; find evolutionarily stable strategies. |
| 13 | Modeling & its limits | all | Fit models of rising complexity; plot the bias-variance tradeoff out of sample. |

Shared tools: Monte Carlo simulation, agent-based modeling, Bayesian inference, information theory, dynamical systems.
